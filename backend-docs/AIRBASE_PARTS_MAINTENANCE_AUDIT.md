# Airbase, Parts, and Maintenance-History Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-16  
**Scope:** read-only inspection of the integrated repository, generated CSV fixtures, HTML parser/import paths, maintenance models, ML feature/scoring code, and isolated scheduling prototype. No web pages were fetched and no database was opened or written.

## 1. Executive summary

The integrated backend contains two manual HTML import paths, but neither provides airbase records, facility-specific inventory, flight hours to a base, or time-specific availability. The repository contains six fabricated inventory rows and four fabricated maintenance-history rows. These are explicitly generated demonstration data, not scraped operational records. The local `backend/db.sqlite3` is present but is zero bytes, so this audit could not verify any persisted database rows.

Consequently, **zero real/imported airbases and zero scraped parts/assets can be selected**. A small six-item parts sample is available only from the clearly labeled fabricated inventory CSV. It has no base association. No trustworthy source supplies flight hours to reach a candidate base. Maintenance schema fields can represent some dates and completed work orders, but the local database has no records and the fixtures are synthetic; there is no verified operational last-maintenance history or approved interval source.

`recommend_base_slots()` is an isolated synthetic prototype. Its inputs can exercise parts-at-base evidence, fleet-specific flight hours, occupancy, a fixed 72-hour duration, and a three-result cap, but no current scraped or persisted records can populate those inputs truthfully. No scheduler code or tests were changed for this audit.

## 5. Parts-to-airbase associations

No inspected scraped source links a part to an airbase. `inventory.csv` has no base/location field, and `InventoryPart` has no facility relationship. A work order may refer to one `required_part`, but the relationship is work-order-to-global-catalogue item, not work-order-to-base stock; it also has no required quantity. `MaintenanceRecommendation.required_part` has the same global catalogue relationship.

The synthetic ML generator's optional `facilities.csv` output includes a semicolon-separated `parts_available` subsystem list. This is generated demonstration content, not a scraped source, and lists subsystem labels rather than inventory part IDs or quantities. It cannot be joined into trustworthy stock evidence.

**Conclusion:** base-specific part availability is unsupported by current real/imported data and schema. Missing links must remain unknown, never be interpreted as stock available.

## 6. Flight-hours data availability

No inspected source provides elapsed flight hours required for a particular aircraft/fleet to reach a candidate airbase.

- `fleet.Aircraft.total_flight_hours` is cumulative aircraft usage, not travel time to a destination.
- `squadron.SquadronAircraft.flight_hours` is also cumulative; it is not route-specific.
- Fabricated aircraft/telemetry fixtures contain cumulative or per-observation usage values, not dispatch/travel estimates.
- The HTML scrapers contain no route, distance, duration, origin/destination, or dispatch information.
- The synthetic scheduler requires `flight_hours_to_base` explicitly associated with the requested `fleet_id`, but no repository-backed operational source supplies it.

Do not derive travel hours from aircraft totals, coordinates, straight-line distance, or assumed speed. A future source would need to provide a validated fleet/aircraft-to-base travel-time value, units, method/source, observation time, validity/coverage, and mapping identifiers.

## 7. Last-maintenance schema and record findings

### Schema

- `fleet.WorkOrder.completed_at` is nullable and `WorkOrder.status` includes `resolved` and `cancelled`; the model also has aircraft/component links, `created_at`, optional `downtime_hours`, and outcome text. It has no maintenance classification (scheduled/preventive/corrective), no interval, no completion verifier/source, and no explicit completion-event integrity rule. A resolved status or populated date is not, on its own, verified evidence that a periodic maintenance requirement was met.
- `fleet.AircraftComponent.last_serviced_at` is nullable, but is a date field without a linked maintenance event or source provenance. `Subsystem.service_interval_days` has a default of 180, and `service_interval_cycles` is optional. The default is schema configuration, not evidence of an approved aircraft maintenance interval.
- `maintenance.AssetPart.last_maintained_on` and `next_maintenance_on` are nullable dates on a serialized installed part. They do not constitute a verified aircraft maintenance event or base inventory record; provenance and completion verification are not represented.
- `maintenance.MaintenanceTask.scheduled_for` is a planned timestamp. Its status can be pending, in progress, completed, or cancelled, but the model has no completed-at timestamp or relation to `WorkOrder`. Do not use `created_at` or `scheduled_for` as a completed maintenance date.
- `squadron.SquadronAircraft.last_maintenance_on` and `next_maintenance_on` are nullable dates, but the records are not populated by the inspected Bharat-Rakshak parser and are not linked by FK to `fleet.Aircraft`.
- `fleet.MaintenanceRecommendation.due_by` is a recommendation deadline, not evidence that maintenance was completed.

## 8. Scheduled/preventive-maintenance readiness

The repository can calculate synthetic model features from `last_service_date`: `ml/src/aeropulse_ml/data/features.py` computes `days_since_service` and a `maintenance_overdue` indicator against `service_interval_days` loaded from `ml/configs/scoring.yaml` (`180`). `ml/src/aeropulse_ml/scoring.py` also exposes a due score. This is part of the existing ML feature/scoring contract; it is not a verified maintenance-history service and was not changed here. A neutral score when service history is missing is not evidence that the aircraft is up to date.

For real work orders, current available fields do not jointly prove the latest completed maintenance event, its classification, the applicable approved interval, or the record's provenance. Therefore scheduled-maintenance state is **unknown** for operational data.

Future transparent status semantics should use the latest *verified completed* event for the specific aircraft/component and a separately approved interval as of a stated time. Missing/ambiguous history or interval means `unknown`. `due soon` requires an explicitly approved lead threshold; none was found. The subsystem 180-day default and ML scoring configuration must not be repurposed as aviation maintenance policy without approval. No status or model score was changed in this task.

## 10. Compatibility with `recommend_base_slots()`

`dev/maintenance_slot_prototype/simple_scheduler.py` accepts an explicit synthetic `WorkOrder`, candidate `BaseEvidence` records, and a UTC-aware search range. Each base fixture supplies:

- Required-part quantities and a parts evidence snapshot.
- `flight_hours_to_base` mapped to the requested fleet and a flight-hours snapshot.
- Occupancy intervals and an occupancy evidence snapshot.

The prototype validates synthetic provenance, completeness, freshness and horizon coverage; requires the part quantity at the base; uses half-open interval overlap against a different fleet; evaluates exactly 72 elapsed hours; samples candidate starts on a grid; ranks eligible candidates by flight hours, then start and base ID; and returns no more than three. It does not book, reserve, approve, mutate a database, or call Django. The existing `recommend_slots()` contract remains separate.

The repository's available scraper output cannot safely populate `BaseEvidence`: airbase identities, base-specific parts quantities, route flight hours, and occupancy evidence are absent. Current global fixture stock and synthetic ML facility-ranking values must not be substituted. Thus the function is compatible with explicitly labeled test fixtures, but not currently usable for real candidate availability.

## 11. Missing fields and relationships

For a future source-backed implementation, the current project lacks:

1. An airbase/facility entity with stable source identity, canonical name/location, timezone if relevant, and mapping/provenance.
2. A part-stock location relation tying part ID and usable quantity to a particular base, including reservations/unavailable stock and observation/validity metadata.
3. A task-level required-parts collection with explicit part IDs and quantities (current work order has at most one optional FK and no quantity).
4. Fleet identity and a trustworthy mapping from aircraft/work order to the occupancy unit; `fleet.WorkOrder` identifies an aircraft, while `Squadron` and `SquadronAircraft` are separate models without a canonical fleet/base occupancy relation.
5. A base occupancy/reservation/commitment source with explicit half-open intervals and complete coverage.
6. Fleet/aircraft-to-base travel-hours data with units, provenance, observation time and coverage.
7. A maintenance event record that distinguishes completed from planned/cancelled work, identifies type, completion time, aircraft/component, verifier and source.
8. An approved task/component maintenance interval and due-soon threshold with provenance and applicability.

## 12. Minimal recommended future data contract

Keep the existing scheduler isolated until sources are approved. A future adapter should provide, without defaults:

- **Airbase:** stable source/base ID, mapped AeroPulse ID, name/location, source/version, observed time, validity/coverage interval, explicit completeness and validation state.
- **Base stock:** base ID + part ID + usable quantity + unit; reserved/quarantined treatment; snapshot/record IDs; observed time; coverage/validity and completeness; correction/cancellation semantics.
- **Work-order requirements:** work-order ID, aircraft/component and resolved fleet/occupancy identity, required part IDs and positive quantities, and requirement provenance.
- **Travel time:** aircraft/fleet ID + origin/base ID + elapsed flight hours, units and method; source record/version, observed time, validity/coverage and completeness. Missing or mismatched identity is unknown.
- **Occupancy:** base ID + stable commitment ID + fleet ID + timezone-aware UTC start/end; status/cancellation/correction; source snapshot and complete coverage through the candidate horizon.
- **Maintenance event:** stable event/work-order ID, aircraft/component ID, explicit maintenance type, completed state, completion instant/date and verifier/source provenance. Planned or merely recommended tasks must remain distinguishable.
- **Maintenance policy:** approved interval and units, applicability to aircraft/component/task type, source/version and effective period; separate approved `due soon` threshold. Do not infer either from training/scoring config.

An adapter must fail closed when IDs cannot be mapped or evidence is stale, incomplete, contradictory or missing. This is a proposal, not an existing schema or feed.

## 13. Synthetic/non-operational limitations

- Scraper code and hard-coded source URLs are not evidence that current pages were fetched or that imported rows exist.
- The six listed stock rows, four maintenance rows, 30 aircraft rows, telemetry, and risk labels are fabricated fixtures. They must not be presented as real inventory, maintenance history, or operational telemetry.
- The synthetic ML facility generator and heuristic `rank_facilities()` do not prove base stock, travel hours, or slot occupancy.
- No operational availability, flight-hours, maintenance due state, or feasible base can be claimed from this repository snapshot.
- The 72-hour duration and one-fleet-per-base overlap are prototype rules only. A recommendation remains advisory and requires human review; no automatic booking or approval exists.
